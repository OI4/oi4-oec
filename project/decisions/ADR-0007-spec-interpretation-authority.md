# ADR-0007 – Product owner decides spec interpretations

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-12
- Deciders: product owner (Thomas), C-003
- Related: Q-004, G-3.5

## Decision

Interpretations of ambiguous or contradictory guideline sections (`SPEC-FINDINGS.md`) are decided by the product owner. Agents propose an interpretation; the decision is recorded in the finding (`decided (PO, C-xxx)`) and, if it has protocol impact, in an ADR. Findings are later handed to the OI4 work group as feedback (status `reported`), but implementation does not wait for the work group.
