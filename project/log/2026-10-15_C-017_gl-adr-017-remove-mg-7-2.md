## Intent

PO request (chat, 2026-10-15): MG 7.2.1 and 7.2.2 no longer make sense and are misleading. Write a guideline ADR 017 ("adapt Minimum Set of Topic Elements to the new compliance levels"), remove the chapter – it follows from the compliance levels – and describe where it fits: a device either interacts directly or is represented by another service; in both cases it (or the service) fulfills a defined level; a service that offers information for individual devices must match its own compliance level for them. Probably in MG 1.3 rather than in the topics chapter.

The planned area cycle IL + TOP moves to C-018 (later P1 cycles shift by one).
