## Intent

The PO reviewed the C-014 increment and decided the open findings (chat, 2026-10-15):

1. `oec/spec/requirements/view/cmp.md` and the CMP entries of `oec/spec/coverage/informative.yaml` are accepted. Question: is every CMP requirement contained in the guideline, or is a spec finding recorded for it?
2. **SF-037:** not every service is configured, subscribes or provides data – that was the idea before the compliance levels. At **Level 3 all messages are mandatory**. A service without `Config`, `Data` or subscriptions answers a request with an **empty list** and publishes nothing on its own as long as it has no values – it simply reacts to requests. Build this into the requirements and record it as proposed interpretation of SF-037.
3. **SF-038:** separate MG and RIG clearly. A microcontroller can reach OEC Messaging Compliance Level 3 without being compliant to the RIG (it is not a container, so not even Level 1) – that is fine. A RIG Level 3 component may have Messaging Level 1 only. Record this definition as proposed interpretation.
4. Before continuing, the guideline is updated accordingly – the agent edits `oi4-oec-documentation` directly – and the guideline register `oi4-oec-documentation/findings/SPEC-FINDINGS.md` is aligned with `project/SPEC-FINDINGS.md`.
