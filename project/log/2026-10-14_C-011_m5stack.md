## Analysis

M5Stack controllers are based on the ESP32 family:
- Core/Core2: ESP32
- CoreS3/AtomS3: ESP32-S3

They are programmed with Arduino (`M5Unified`), ESP-IDF or UIFlow. **No architecture change is needed**, because the ESP-IDF reference port and the planned Arduino port already cover them.

## Changes (ADR-0017 amendment C-011)

- `esp32s3` moves from best effort to **supported**.
- **M5Stack Core2 or CoreS3** becomes a second reference device, whichever the PO has available.
- An additional **M5Stack example firmware** uses the display and buttons via `M5Unified` (MIT), in the example only, not in the SDK.
- UIFlow/MicroPython is out of scope.
