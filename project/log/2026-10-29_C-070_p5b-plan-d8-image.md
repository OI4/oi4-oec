## Work – increment 1 (`image`)

### 1. OCI and SBOM readers (`ff3ef83`)

- `internal/oci`: reads an OCI image layout directory or an uncompressed oci-archive (tar of a layout); verifies the digest and size of every blob it reads (index, manifests, configs, layers); follows nested indexes; Docker schema-2 media types accepted, schema 1 is a structure problem; attestation manifests (`vnd.docker.reference.type: attestation-manifest`) and `unknown/unknown` platforms are not runnable images; layers gzip or uncompressed, zstd reported as unreadable; `FS` applies the layers in order (whiteouts and opaque directories, hard links, symlinks resolved inside the image root, `..` clamped).
- `internal/oci/ocitest`: test-only builder of deterministic OCI archives (fixed times, sorted entries) with knobs for every fault (digest mismatch, sha512, platforms, layers, configs).
- `internal/sbom`: minimal readers of SPDX 2.2/2.3 JSON and CycloneDX 1.x JSON (tolerant of unknown keys), license expressions to identifiers (SPDX expression grammar: `AND`/`OR`/`WITH`, parentheses, `+`), `LicenseRef-`/`DocumentRef-`, CycloneDX `license.id`/`name`/`text`/`expression`.

## Risks and open technical points

- `docker save` writes an OCI layout only on Docker 25+ (containerd image store or 25+ classic); older versions produce a Docker archive → exit 2 with the hint.
- T8-IMG-03 checks every runnable platform image; a multi-arch image with many platforms is read completely (memory bounded by the largest layer entry, files are not kept except under `/opt/oi4/licenses`).
- The Windows named pipe of the Engine API (increment 4) may need `golang.org/x/sys` or `Microsoft/go-winio`; decided then.

## Addendum 1 – PO review: D-1 and R-1 (2026-10-29)

PO answer (verbatim): "D-1, uns ist nur wichtig, dass alle Lizenzen Lizenz Konform genannt werden, zudem soll das Ganze "CRA und RED Ready" sein. Die Versionen möchte ich nicht einschränken, da sich diese über die Zeit ja ändern, die Guideline aber länger bestand haben könnte. Zu den Texten, es muss einfach rechtskonform sein. R-1 akzeptiert"

- **R-1 accepted:** the 13 image fixtures in `oec/conformance/testdata/images/index.yaml` are `reviewed`.
- **D-1 answered, not as proposed.** The answer sets four goals for RIG 4.1.4:
  1. no restriction of the SPDX/CycloneDX versions (proposal (a) dropped);
  2. every license named in a license-conformant way;
  3. the SBOM "CRA and RED ready";
  4. license texts as the law requires.
- Goals 2–4 need guideline text that a tool can check. That text is the PO's decision (G-3.4 a). So SF-114 gets a **revised proposal** in the register, and the exact wording goes back to the PO for confirmation (D-1 revised). Facts behind the revision:
  - CRA Annex I Part II (1) asks for an SBOM "in a commonly used and machine-readable format covering at the very least the top-level dependencies".
  - The RED delegated regulation (EU) 2022/30 and EN 18031 name no SBOM, but expect an inventory of the software components for the handling of vulnerabilities.
  - Most licenses require their text to accompany the software. A tool cannot decide per license whether a text is legally needed, so a text for every named license and exception is the testable rule that is always legally compliant.
- Until the PO confirms, D8 keeps the C-070 behaviour. The two fixtures that depend on SF-114 (`invalid-sbom`, `missing-license-text`) will be reworked with the decision and go back to `draft`.
