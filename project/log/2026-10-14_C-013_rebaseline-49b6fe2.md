## New findings

- **SF-035 – behavior stated without keyword.** Under MG 1.2, keyword-less statements are informative. Many protocol-defining behaviors in MG 6–9 have no keyword: Health on init/change/every 60 s (MG 7.1.5.1:20, no keyword anywhere), Birth topic (MG 6.3.3:8), Status event after `Set`/`Del` (MG 9.1.3:164, 9.1.4:154, 9.1.5:86, 9.1.8:213, 9.1.9:134/178, 9.1.10:134/202), pagination continuation (MG 9.1.1–9.1.10), PublicationList `Mode` semantics (MG 8.3.8:82–94). 37 hits for "will publish / is published / publishes as many" alone. Proposal: PO adds keywords (preferred); interim rule G-1.6 (treat as MUST, flag `implicit`).
- **SF-036 – closed set of resources/services.** After GL-ADR-014 no sentence says whether other resource/service names below `Oi4v2` are allowed. MG 7.1.1:12 "All topics starting with the Namespace element shall be OI4 Alliance-conform" suggests a closed set; MG 7.1.5.2 still says "For well-defined services, the service name is equal to the name of the Action", a remnant implying other services. Proposal: closed set; reword the sentence.
