## Decisions / assumptions (agent, G-4.4)

- Keyword-less statements that define the mechanisms the MG 6 note declares mandatory are implicit MUSTs (G-1.6, SF-057); `implicit: true` with refs SF-035 and SF-057, as in TOP.
- "preferably shall" (MG 6.2.2) and "should be sufficient" (MG 6.3.1) read as SHOULD (SF-057 (d), (e)).
- The Close-message rules of MG 6.3.4 are graded (MG 6.3.4 is not in the list of the MG 6 note), the Will interpretation (MG 6.3.5) is not – so REQ-LIFE-011 (Close interpretation) is `{SHOULD, SHOULD, MUST}`, REQ-LIFE-012 (Will interpretation) MUST at all levels.
- The subscriber rules (REQ-LIFE-011 … 013) carry the condition "the subscriber tracks the online state of other applications" (SF-059 (b)).
- REQ-LIFE-014 (reserved Health values, MUST NOT at all levels) is derived from the subscriber rules (SF-059 (a)); a HealthScore 0 with another Health state stays allowed.
- Birth/Close/Will use the DataSetWriterId of the regular `MAM`/`Health` publication about the application itself (REQ-MSG-021: one DataSetWriterId per resource), as in B1.1.1/B1.1.2.
- REQ-MQTT-008 (verify the broker certificate) is a security consequence of MG 6.1/6.2.2 + RIG 4.1.1/4.1.2 (GUARDRAILS G-4.5), proposed in SF-060 (b).
- MQTT v5.0 allowed but not depended upon (SF-060 (a)); ClientId of a component without container (SF-060 (c)) only as note of REQ-MQTT-013.
- The B1 notes "shall not use SequenceNumber/Timestamp" are not taken over (MG 1.2: examples are informative); REQ-LIFE-004/010 keep the SHOULD NOT of MG 6.3.3/6.3.5 (SF-058 (b)).
- Verification methods: D7 needs a controlled test broker for reconnect/ungraceful disconnect/certificate checks (`d7-test`); broker/platform rules are `d8`.
