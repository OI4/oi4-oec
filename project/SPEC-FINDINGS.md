# Spec findings – guideline v2.0 draft

Ambiguities, contradictions and gaps found in the guideline (`oi4-oec-documentation`).
**Current baseline: `5f070bb` (2026-10-14)** – Messaging Guideline (MG, `docs/guideline/`) and Runtime & Infrastructure Guideline (RIG, `docs/infrastructure-guideline/`), with all guideline ADRs (GL-ADR-001 … 011) implemented. SF-001 … SF-022 were raised against `c85ce2a` and re-checked in C-006. Locations are given in the new numbering.

Each finding gets a proposed interpretation. The PO decides (ADR-0007); a decision with protocol impact also gets an ADR.

Status: `open` → `decided` (PO, cycle) → `reported` (handed to the work group) · `resolved` (fixed in the guideline) · `obsolete` (no longer applicable after a GL-ADR)
