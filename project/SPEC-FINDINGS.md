# Spec findings – guideline v2.0 draft

Ambiguities, contradictions and gaps found in the guideline (`oi4-oec-documentation`).
**Current baseline: `7bead4c` (branch `feature/spec-findings`, 2026-10-14)** – Messaging Guideline (MG, `docs/guideline/`) and Runtime & Infrastructure Guideline (RIG, `docs/infrastructure-guideline/`) with GL-ADR-001 … 013.

The PO resolves findings directly in the guideline repo; the guideline repo mirrors this register in `oi4-oec-documentation/findings/SPEC-FINDINGS.md`. **SF ids are shared between both registers – the next free id is SF-035.** New findings are added here first (with location, proposed interpretation) and reported to the PO.

Each finding gets a proposed interpretation. The PO decides (ADR-0007); a decision with protocol impact also gets an ADR (ours) or a GL-ADR (guideline).

Status: `open` → `decided` (PO, cycle) → `reported` (handed to the work group) · `resolved` (fixed in the guideline) · `obsolete` (no longer applicable after a GL-ADR)

| SF-032 | Normative keywords | Neither MG nor RIG defines the meaning of normative words (no reference to RFC 2119/8174 or the ISO/IEC Directives). "shall" and "must" are both used (MG 107×/136×, RIG 25×/27×). The admonition types of MG 1.2 do not match the strength of their content: 12 `RECOMMENDED` boxes say "the operator **must** prevent this via ACL" (e.g. MG 10.1.3, 10.3.1); `HIGHLY RECOMMENDED` boxes contain hard rules (MG 4.1 "with and without scheme are **different** ILs", MG 4.2 "standard Base64 alphabet **must not** be used"). The REQ catalog needs a defined MUST/SHOULD/MAY per requirement. | MG 1.2 (and all chapters), RIG | For the catalog: (1) *shall*, *must*, *mandatory*, *required*, level table value "Mandatory", field attribute `Requirement: Mandatory` → **MUST**; *shall not*, *must not* → **MUST NOT**. (2) *should*, *recommended*, *best practice*, level table value "Recommended", admonitions `RECOMMENDED`/`HIGHLY RECOMMENDED` without a keyword → **SHOULD**; *should not* → **SHOULD NOT**. (3) *may*, *can*, *optional*, field attribute `Requirement: Optional` → **MAY**; field attribute `Requirement: Not used` → **MUST NOT** (key absent). (4) The keyword in the sentence wins over the admonition type. (5) Format definitions of fields (Type, Value Range) without keyword → **MUST**. (6) Statements addressed to the operator/platform → domain `platform`, not tested on applications. Suggestion for the work group: add a conventions paragraph to MG 1.2 / RIG (RFC 2119 + RFC 8174 keywords or ISO/IEC Directives Part 2 verbal forms) and align box types. | open (C-012) |

MG 1.3 says Level 3 "requires the complete implementation" of the guideline, but it is not stated whether immature chapters are part of it. MG 8.1.5.3 lists the specific services as topic resources. | MG 1.3, 6.1.3, 6.2.3, 8.1.5.3, 10.3 | Requirements from immature chapters are cataloged with `maturity: immature`, keyword at most **MAY**, and are **not part of the compliance verdict at any level** (D7 may report them informatively). Their topic syntax (`Call`/`Reply` + service name) is still validated if such messages appear. MG 6 is informative (process description) anyway. Revisit when the work group matures the chapters. | open (C-012) |
